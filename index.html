ABOUT THE SPORT

What is Formula 1?

Formula 1, commonly known as F1, is the highest level of international single-seater motor racing. It is one of the most technologically advanced and competitive sports in the world.

Drivers compete in specially designed racing cars on circuits around the world. Every race requires incredible speed, concentration, teamwork and strategy.

The Formula 1 World Championship began in 1950. Since then, the sport has grown into a global competition watched by millions of fans.

THE RACE WEEKEND

How Does an F1 Weekend Work?

01. Practice

Drivers practise on the circuit to learn its layout and help their teams improve the car's setup.

02. Qualifying

Drivers compete to set the fastest lap times. Their results determine the starting order for the race.

03. Race Day

Drivers compete over a set race distance. The first driver to complete the race wins the Grand Prix.

DID YOU KNOW?

Formula 1 Facts

  1. The first Formula 1 World Championship season took place in 1950.
  2. F1 cars can travel at speeds exceeding 350 km/h.
  3. Each team has two full-time race drivers.
  4. There are races held on several continents.
  5. Teams use advanced computers to analyse racing data.
